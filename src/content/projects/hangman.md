## Overview

A simple hangman game made with **.NET MAUI**, featuring a variety of developer jargon, a user-friendly interface and cross-platform support for both mobile and desktop.

## Why I built it

It started as an assignment for my Mobile Development class. I kept developing it to teach myself animations and game development in C#.

## Status

Still in its first iteration. I plan to complete it by the end of the year.
